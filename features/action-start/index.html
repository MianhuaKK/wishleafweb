03 / A few useful details

Before you begin.

What to expect

  • AI proposals can be inaccurate or unsuitable. Review the content, scope, and dates before adopting one.
  • Task assistance uses the AI allowances available to your account. Current eligibility and Pro options are shown in the app.
  • Your hand-created tasks and existing records remain available when AI allowances run out.
How is Suggest different from Break down?

Suggest offers practical ways to approach or begin a task. Break down proposes a structure of smaller tasks, which may be organized by time, activity, or sequence. You can choose the kind of help that suits the situation.

What if smaller steps make me feel more overwhelmed?

Try Suggest to explore another starting strategy, or give feedback on a breakdown that doesn't fit. You decide which proposals to adopt. A longer checklist is only useful if it helps you act.

Does WishLeaf change my tasks automatically?

Task-assistance proposals are presented for review. Choosing and adopting a proposal applies it to the task or creates subtasks. Review the proposed change before confirming it.

Do I need to set a goal first?

No. You can create an everyday task and use task assistance on its own. If you have a longer-term goal, you can associate the task with it.

Can I use WishLeaf for free?

WishLeaf is free to download with in-app purchases. Manual tasks, manual goals, and existing records remain usable independently of AI allowances. AI features have account-specific allowances or Pro access; check the current details in the app.
