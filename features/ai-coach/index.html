03 / A few useful details

Before you start chatting.

Where do I find AI Coach?

Open Goals and tap the AI Coach icon on a goal card. The conversation belongs to that goal, so you can return to it as you work through your next steps.

Does a suggested task go straight onto my list?

A proposed task starts as a draft marked “Not scheduled · confirmation needed.” Review it and tap Add task to create it. Suggestions you haven't confirmed remain drafts in the conversation.

Can I ask for a different suggestion?

Yes. Keep chatting about what doesn't fit, how much time you have, or what you'd like to try instead. Review any new drafts before adding them.

Is the conversation on this page live?

This page uses a scripted example so you can try sending a follow-up and adding draft tasks. Nothing is sent to an AI service or saved to an account. Open WishLeaf for a conversation about your own goal.

What should I know about AI access?

AI Coach uses the AI access available to your account. Current allowances and Pro options are shown in the app. AI-generated content can be inaccurate; review suggestions and adjust them to your situation before using them.
