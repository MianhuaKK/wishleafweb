What “Days” means

The calendar-day span from goal creation to its most recent activity, including both dates. It describes the goal's recorded journey; it is neither consecutive completion days nor a count of days with activity.

What “Actions” means

The cumulative number of recorded actions associated with the goal. Use it to see your contributions alongside the actual records and milestones.

These numbers describe time and recorded contributions. To understand a change in skill or ability, look at the work itself, your experience, and the milestones you've reached.

    Know what you're looking at

    • Goal maps contain records linked to that goal. A standalone task is not automatically added to every map.
    • Action counts and date spans describe recorded effort. They do not measure your intelligence, discipline, or future potential.
    • AI reflections depend on account allowances and product conditions. Review interpretations against your own experience.
    Do I need to maintain a daily streak?

    No compulsory daily streak is used for the goal card's Days and Actions figures. Days is the inclusive span from goal creation to the latest activity; Actions is a cumulative count. You can return to the same goal after a break and add to its history.

    Does every task appear on my goal map?

    A task appears on a goal's map when it is associated with that goal. You can also use tasks and life records independently, without creating a long-term goal first.

    Does a higher action count mean my ability has improved?

    The count tells you how many actions were recorded for the goal. Assess changes in ability using the work you produced, relevant milestones, and your own observations. Your present activity also doesn't define what you'll be able to do in the future.

    Can I change the route after I start?

    Yes. You can edit unfinished milestones or review an AI replan. Completed milestones are retained through later editing and AI replanning. AI blueprint creation and replanning use Blueprint Tickets or Pro access; the current balance and terms are shown in the app.

    What happens when my AI allowance runs out?

    Manual tasks, manual goals, goal maps, and existing records remain available. Basic daily statistics and your own notes can still be saved, while AI-generated highlights and other AI content may be unavailable. Weekly Insight has separate activity and AI eligibility conditions.

    Can I track financial results or automatic progress percentages?

    The experience described here covers milestones, linked actions, counts, and reflection. It doesn't promise a financial dashboard, an automatic goal-completion percentage, or a forecast of when you'll achieve a result.
